1. Brand & Visual Direction (Priority: 120% design quality)
Target audience: women, primarily 18–35, fashion-conscious
Aesthetic: modern, feminine, premium boutique feel — warm, earthy, elevated tones: cream, light brown, beige, and warm tan/terracotta accents, NOT the loud red/orange SHEIN uses. This should feel soft, natural, and boutique-luxe (think high-end minimalist fashion brand, not a discount fast-fashion site)
Use cream/beige as the dominant background tone, light brown/tan for accents, buttons, and highlights, and a deep brown or charcoal for text to keep contrast readable
Typography: elegant serif or clean modern sans-serif for headings (e.g. Playfair Display or Cormorant for logo/headings), clean sans-serif for body (e.g. Inter, Poppins)
Generous white space, soft shadows, rounded corners on cards, smooth hover animations (image zoom, quick-view fade-in)
Sticky header featuring the brand logo (use a stock/placeholder icon for now — e.g. a minimalist boutique/fashion-related icon such as a hanger, shopping bag, or monogram-style icon from a standard icon library like Font Awesome or Lucide — paired with the text "THEXIAS_PLACE" in the brand's heading font), search bar, and cart icon with live item count
Structure the logo so it can easily be swapped for a final custom logo file later (single reusable Logo component/asset reference, not hardcoded inline everywhere)
Hero banner section on homepage with rotating promotional slides (placeholder text like "New Arrivals", "Shop the Look")
Fully mobile-responsive — most traffic will be mobile
